PyDevices / micropython-hardware

micropython-hardware

The canonical PyDevices product source: portable displaydev and audiodev, optional eventsys, timers, event primitives, board configs, and hardware drivers. Install unprefixed packages with mip on MicroPython, or namespaced pydevices-* distributions from TestPyPI on CPython. The pydisplay repo shows the product in complete examples, PyScript apps, and notebooks.
